## Matter

## Facts given

Written statement for the defendant. Defendant: Sample Person 2, child of Sample Person 4, aged 50, resident of Plot 000, Sample Nagar, Mysuru, in O.S. No. 000 of 2026 before the District Court, Mysuru, filed by the plaintiff Sample Person 1 for recovery of Rs 5,00,000 said to have been lent on 10.01.2025. The defendant took only Rs 2,00,000 from the plaintiff and repaid it fully by bank transfer on 15.06.2025 (UTR no. SAMPLE-UTR-000). The defendant denies taking Rs 5,00,000 and denies that anything is due. Summons was served on the defendant on 05.10.2026. Documents: the defendant's bank statement showing the transfer of 15.06.2025. The defendant asks that the suit be dismissed with costs.
