## Matter

T-691 A/B, run 5 #3b (v2 copy): partition among four children, three defendants (Ludhiana)

## Facts given

Suit for partition. Plaintiff: Sample Person 1, child of late Sample Person 5, aged 39, resident of Plot 000, Sample Nagar, Ludhiana. Defendants: (1) Sample Person 2, (2) Sample Person 6 and (3) Sample Person 7, all children of late Sample Person 5 and residents of Plot 000, Sample Nagar, Ludhiana. Sample Person 5 died intestate on 02.03.2024, leaving these four children as the only heirs. The property is the father's self-acquired house at Plot 000, Sample Nagar, Ludhiana, and a shop at Plot 001, Sample Nagar, Ludhiana. The plaintiff claims a one-fourth share. Defendant No. 1 occupies the shop and keeps its income; all four live in the house. The plaintiff asked for partition on 15.07.2026 and the defendants refused on 01.08.2026. The property is valued at Rs 30,00,000 and court fee of Rs 1,500 is paid.
