# D17

## Matter

T-691 A/B, run 5 #1 (v2 copy): declaration of title and cancellation of a forged sale deed (Lucknow)

## Facts given

Suit for declaration of title and cancellation of a sale deed. Plaintiff: Sample Person 1, child of Sample Person 3, aged 60, resident of Plot 000, Sample Nagar, Lucknow. Defendant: Sample Person 2, child of Sample Person 4, resident of Plot 001, Sample Nagar, Lucknow. The plaintiff owns agricultural plot no. 002, Sample Nagar, District Lucknow, inherited from the plaintiff's father Sample Person 3, who died on 14.02.2011; the plaintiff's name is in the khatauni and the plaintiff is in possession. On 03.08.2026 the plaintiff learnt from a new khatauni extract that a sale deed dated 12.02.2025 had been registered in the defendant's favour over this plot. The plaintiff never signed that sale deed; the plaintiff's signature on it is forged. The plaintiff asks for a declaration that the plaintiff is the owner of the plot and that the sale deed dated 12.02.2025 is void and not binding on the plaintiff, and for its cancellation. Valuation for jurisdiction Rs 3,00,000; court fee paid on that valuation. The cause of action arose on 03.08.2026, when the plaintiff learnt of the deed. The plot lies within the jurisdiction of this court. Documents: the khatauni extracts, the death certificate of the plaintiff's father and a certified copy of the sale deed dated 12.02.2025.
