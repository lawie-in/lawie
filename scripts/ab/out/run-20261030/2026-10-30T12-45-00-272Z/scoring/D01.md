# D01

## Matter

T-691 A/B, O.39 R.1-2 temporary injunction (Patna): the triple test argued from the facts given

## Facts given

Application under Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 CPC in Title Suit No. 000 of 2026, pending before the Civil Judge (Senior Division), Patna. Plaintiff: Sample Person 1 (the applicant), child of Sample Person 3, aged 52, resident of Plot 000, Sample Nagar, Patna. Defendant: Sample Person 2 (the non-applicant), child of Sample Person 4, resident of Plot 001, Sample Nagar, Patna. Suit property: Plot 002, Sample Nagar, Patna, 1,200 sq ft with a boundary wall, bought by the plaintiff under a registered sale deed dated 10.01.2019; the plaintiff is in possession. The main suit asks for a declaration of the plaintiff's title to the suit property and a permanent injunction restraining the defendant from interfering with the plaintiff's possession. On 05.10.2026 the defendant brought bricks and labourers to the plot, and on 12.10.2026 the defendant began digging a foundation along the eastern boundary, saying the plot belongs to the defendant. The plaintiff complained at PS Sample Nagar on 13.10.2026. Relief now sought: a prohibitory temporary injunction restraining the defendant, and anyone acting for the defendant, from entering the suit property, raising any construction on it, and transferring or creating any third-party interest in it until the suit is decided. Ex parte ad-interim relief is not sought. Prima facie case: the plaintiff holds the registered sale deed of 10.01.2019 and the mutation and rent receipts are in the plaintiff's name; the defendant has shown no document. Balance of convenience: the plaintiff is in possession and the defendant loses nothing if things stay as they are, while construction would change the property. Irreparable injury: a construction on the plot and any transfer would alter the suit property and create third-party rights that money cannot undo. The plaintiff undertakes to compensate the defendant for any loss if the injunction is later found to have been wrongly obtained. Documents: certified copy of the sale deed dated 10.01.2019, the mutation order, rent receipts for 2025-26, photographs of the digging taken on 12.10.2026 and a copy of the police complaint dated 13.10.2026.
