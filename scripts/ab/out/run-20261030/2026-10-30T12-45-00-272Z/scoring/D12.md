# D12

## Matter

T-691 A/B, run 5 #3a (v2 copy): partition between two siblings, one defendant (Patna)

## Facts given

Suit for partition. Plaintiff: Sample Person 1, child of late Sample Person 3, aged 48, resident of Plot 000, Sample Nagar, Patna. Defendant: Sample Person 2, child of late Sample Person 3, aged 45, the plaintiff's sibling, of the same address. Their father Sample Person 3 died intestate on 10.05.2020; their mother died before him, and the two children are his only heirs. The joint family property is a two-storey house on Plot 000, Sample Nagar, Patna, and 1 acre of agricultural land at Plot 001, Sample Nagar, District Patna, both ancestral. The plaintiff claims a half share. Both live in the house; the defendant alone collects the rent of the ground floor and cultivates the land. The plaintiff asked for partition by letter dated 01.06.2026 and the defendant refused by reply dated 20.06.2026. The property is valued at Rs 40,00,000 and court fee of Rs 1,500 is paid. The defendant is the only defendant.

Answers on the questions step:
- other_defendants: No, Defendant No. 1 is the only defendant
