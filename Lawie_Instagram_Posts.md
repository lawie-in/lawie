# Lawie — 10 Instagram Posts

**Brand voice:** funny, smart, slightly chaotic, lawyer-roasting. Talks like a friend who happens to know the law. Think *Duolingo + Ramit Sethi + that one friend who actually reads the fine print.*

**Target audience:** freelancers, indie founders, first-time renters, small-biz owners — people who sign things they don't fully understand because hiring a lawyer feels like buying a used car.

**Posting cadence suggestion:** alternate Carousel → Reel → Static, 4–5x/week.

## Posting strategy notes
- **Best times:** Tue–Thu, 11am or 7pm local time (founder/freelancer scroll windows).
- **Engagement bait that actually works:** carousels with "save this" energy + reels with relatable cringe outperform polished hero shots 3:1 on early-stage brands.
- **CTA discipline:** every post points to one thing (link in bio → trial). Don't dilute with multiple asks.
- **First 30 days:** lead with carousels (educational = saves = algo loves it). Reels build reach. Static designs = brand memory.
