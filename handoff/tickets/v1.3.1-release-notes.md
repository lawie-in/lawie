# v1.3.1: what a user can now do (draft, Priya, 30 Oct 2026)

## T-700b: the plaintiff's name in a written statement's Memo of Parties
- Open a written statement's annexures. The Memo of Parties now shows the plaintiff's name exactly as written in the brief, not "[To be confirmed: name of the Plaintiff]".
- If the brief gives no name, or a non-answer such as "Not known", the placeholder still shows.
- Several plaintiffs are listed the same way the Memo lists several defendants.
- Nothing else in the Memo changes.

## T-704 and T-704a: phone layouts
- On a phone, the admin panel opens with a menu button instead of a sidebar that squeezed the page.
- Every admin table scrolls sideways inside its box.
- On the drafting page, the status rows, section pills and cost lines wrap instead of running off the screen.
- The document page's title and back arrow are readable on the dark header.
- The first drafting screen's heading no longer touches the edge of the screen.
- Nothing changes on desktop.

## T-705: revising a draft (DARK in this release: feature.revisions is off in prod until the founder approves the price)
- After a draft is ready, you can ask for a revision.
- The first revision of each draft is free.
- Each later revision costs Ink, at the price the founder sets (Vikram proposes the same Ink as the original draft; not yet approved).

## How Anushka checks it
1. Written statement whose brief names the plaintiff: the Memo of Parties shows that name. With "Not known", it shows the placeholder.
2. On a phone (390 wide): open the admin panel and a document page. The menu button works, tables scroll inside their box, the header title is readable, and nothing runs off the screen. Desktop looks unchanged.
3. Revisions are off: a ready draft shows no Revise button. (After the founder approves and the setting is on: revise a ready draft once: no Ink is charged. Revise it again: the approved price is charged and shown before you confirm.)
