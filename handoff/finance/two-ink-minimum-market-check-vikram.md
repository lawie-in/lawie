## The market

**AI tools sold to Indian advocates:**
- LawCentral: ₹1,000/month "about 110 full drafts" (≈ ₹9 a draft); ₹2,499 and ₹4,900 tiers.
- Claw: ₹799–1,099/month.
- Legal Desk AI: ₹499–999/month, metered by words.
- Juniorlawyer: from ₹1,799/month.
- SCC Online AI Pro: ₹51,500/year (≈ ₹4,300/month).
- Harvey-class tools: $300–800 per seat per month (not sold to solo advocates).

**Research subscriptions they already pay for:** Manupatra about ₹32,000/year; SCC Online about ₹19,000–50,000/year.

**People:**
- A junior advocate: the BCI recommends ₹15,000 (rural) to ₹20,000 (urban) a month, about ₹700–900 per working day.
- An online legal notice: ₹999–2,500. Through an advocate: ₹1,500–7,500.
- Court typists charge per page (state rules, around ₹5–10 a page), for typing only, not drafting.

**Reading:** at ₹22–32 a draft we are 30–80× cheaper than a junior's day or an online notice. Our plan price matches the cheap AI tools (Claw ₹799). On drafts per rupee we lose to LawCentral's claimed ~110 drafts for ₹1,000, if its "full draft" means the same thing ours does. We cannot match ₹9 a draft at our quality: a short draft with a Sonnet extractor costs us about ₹6–7 in model cost alone. We should not compete on draft count. We compete on court-ready output (fact checks, signed legal packs), and the A/B showed that quality needs Sonnet.

## What it does to Solo

At a 2-Ink minimum, 50 Ink buys **25 drafts a month**, down from up to 50. That looks thin next to "110 drafts" claims. Solo has margin headroom: monthly Solo nets ₹13.22 per Ink against about ₹3.5 of cost.

## Conclusion

1. **Confirm Option 1:** Sonnet extractor on every pack, with a 2-Ink minimum. It fixes the dropped facts, and ₹22–32 a draft is still far below any human alternative.
2. **Raise Solo from 50 to 60 Ink a month** (yearly from 600 to 720), price unchanged, so Solo buys **30 drafts**. Margin: Solo monthly 74% → 68% ✅. Solo yearly ₹9.18 per Ink → 62% ✅, on par with Pro yearly. Pro stays at 150.
3. **O.39 → 3 Ink, Partition → 5 Ink.** These are already proposed and deferred to next month under the freeze.
4. **Copy changes:** "short ones 1 Ink" goes; "60 Ink" replaces "50 Ink". Meera and Ajay re-sign. Applies to new purchases only; nobody has bought yet.
5. **Re-check after 2 weeks** of prod data with the Sonnet extractor. If any pack runs above ₹3.70 per Ink at p90, it moves up one Ink.
