# How to use Lawie: help for advocates

This help is for advocates. It is written in plain language, as short questions and answers. Where a question is about the law or about your case strategy, see "What Lawie is not" (section 12).

The disclaimer line, always exact:
Lawie is a drafting assistant. The advocate is responsible for legal accuracy and filing.

---

## 1. What Lawie is

**What is Lawie?**
Lawie is a drafting assistant for Indian advocates. You pick a document or describe what you need, answer a few questions about the matter, then review the draft, edit it and download it in Word or PDF.

**What can I draft?**
More than 90 document types. They include bail applications, legal notices (including notices under s.80 CPC and s.138 NI Act), affidavits, petitions, plaints, family-court matters, and agreements such as rent, NDA, MOU, service, loan and employment agreements. All of them are available on the Free plan, which starts with 5 Ink.

**Which law do the drafts use?**
Criminal drafts use BNS, BNSS and BSA section numbers. Whatever section a draft uses, check it yourself against the bare act before you file.

**What language are the drafts in?**
English. Hindi drafting: coming soon.

**Who is responsible for the draft?**
You are. Every draft carries this line: "Lawie is a drafting assistant. The advocate is responsible for legal accuracy and filing."

---

## 2. Starting a draft

**How do I start a draft?**
Open Lawie after you sign in and start a new document. You may see one of two starting points, depending on your account:

- **Describe it.** A screen asks "What do you need to draft?". You write the matter in your own words. Lawie then asks for the details it still needs, and you check a summary of them ("Check your brief") before the draft is written.
- **Pick a document.** You choose a document from "Browse document types" and fill in a form for the matter.

Either way, you end up with a first draft to review, edit and download.

**Will I see the "describe" screen or the document list?**
You may see either, depending on your account. Both lead to the same place: a first draft for you to review, edit and download.

**What will Lawie ask me?**
It depends on the matter and on what you have already written. Lawie asks for the details it still needs for that document, so the questions differ from one matter to the next. Answer what you can.

**What if I skip a question or don't have a detail yet?**
The draft may show a blank where the detail belongs. See section 5, "Blanks and [To be confirmed]".

**Which document should I pick?**
If you know the document (for example a bail application or a legal notice), choose it from "Browse document types" or name it when you describe the matter. If you are unsure, describe the matter in your own words, where that option is available on your account, or browse the list.

**Does a failed draft cost me anything?**
No. You're charged only after a draft is generated; a failed generation is not charged.

---

## 3. Ink and plans

**What is Ink?**
Ink is Lawie's credit unit. You spend Ink to generate drafts.

**How much Ink do I get to start?**
5 free Ink on sign-up. The Free plan gives you 5 Ink (lifetime). No payment details are needed to sign up.

**How much does a draft cost in Ink?**
Most documents use 2 Ink; short documents, such as most notices and affidavits, use 1. A bail application uses 2 Ink. You're charged only after a draft is generated; a failed generation is not charged.

**Where can I see how much Ink I have?**
In the Ink pill in the top bar. It is there on every page of your dashboard, including the draft screen. It shows your Ink count and your plan, and it has a Top-up button.

**What happens when my Ink runs out?**
You can buy a top-up pack from the Top-up button in the top bar, or choose a monthly plan on the pricing page.

**What does a monthly plan cost?**
Solo is ₹799/month with 50 Ink. A monthly plan refills Ink every month.

**Can I buy extra Ink without a subscription?**
Yes. Top-up packs start from ₹65 for 3 Ink. A Free account can buy a top-up pack too. Top-ups are one-time purchases and need no subscription.

## 5. Blanks and [To be confirmed]

**What is a blank in my draft?**
If Lawie did not have a detail the document needs, the draft shows a blank in the form [To be confirmed: label], where the label says what is missing (for example a date or a name).

**What do I do with a blank?**
Fill it in or remove it before you file. The advocate must fill or remove each [To be confirmed: label] before filing. Search the draft for "To be confirmed" before you download, so none is left behind.

**Why are there blanks at all?**
A detail you did not give, or that the draft could not find, stays as a blank for you to decide. Check every name, date and fact against your case file, whether or not the draft shows a blank.

---

## 6. Editing the draft

**Can I edit the draft?**
Yes. Every line of the draft is yours to change, including the prayer and the verification. Edit it in the editor on the draft screen before you download.

**Is there anything I cannot edit?**
Two things sit outside the editor: the disclaimer banner above it, and the footer on exported files. You cannot remove either one.

**Should I read the whole draft?**
Yes. Read every line. Check every section it cites, and check names, dates, addresses and the facts against your case file. The grounds and the prayer are yours to settle. A short checklist: the sections cited; names, dates and details; the court heading and cause title; the grounds and the prayer; the verification and the advocate block. This list is a start, not a complete one.

---

## 7. Downloading

**What formats can I download?**
Word and PDF.

**Can I keep working in Word?**
Yes. Download the Word file and carry on in the format you already use.

**Do I download before or after editing?**
After. Edit in the editor first, then download.

---

## 8. The disclaimer

**What is the line at the top of my draft?**
It is the disclaimer: "Lawie is a drafting assistant. The advocate is responsible for legal accuracy and filing." It sits in a banner above the editor on every draft. You cannot dismiss it.

**What does it mean?**
Lawie helps with the drafting. You are the advocate. You are responsible for what is in the draft and for filing it.

---

## 9. Referral codes

**How do I use a referral code?**
Type it in the "Have a referral code?" box on the login page when you sign up. Use the code with Google sign-in.

**Who can use a code?**
New accounts only. A code does nothing on an account that already exists.

**My code did nothing. Why?**
An invalid code is silently ignored, so check the spelling and try again on a new sign-up. If you still have trouble, write to contact@lawie.in.

**Can I use a link instead of typing a code?**
No. Type the code in the box.

---

## 10. Signing in

**How do I sign in?**
Sign in with Google. Email and password: coming soon.

**Do I need to enter payment details to sign up?**
No. Sign-up never asks for payment details.

**What do I get when I sign up?**
5 free Ink on sign-up, and access to all document types.

---

## 11. Support and privacy

**Who do I write to for help?**
contact@lawie.in.

**Who do I write to about my data or privacy?**
privacy@lawie.in. Data and privacy requests go there.

---

## 12. What Lawie is not

**Does Lawie give legal advice?**
No. Lawie does not give legal advice and does not file anything. It helps you prepare a first draft. The advocate is responsible for legal accuracy and filing. Lawie is a drafting assistant.

**Can Lawie tell me whether I should file, or what my strategy should be?**
No. Those are your decisions as the advocate. Lawie can help you with the drafting and with using Lawie. For the law and the strategy in a matter, rely on your own reading of the bare act and your judgement. The advocate is responsible for legal accuracy and filing.

**Will Lawie file my document or send it to a court?**
No. You download the draft and file it yourself, the way you always have.

**Is the draft ready to file as it is?**
No. A draft is a first draft for you to review. Read it, check it, fill or remove every [To be confirmed: label], and edit what needs editing. Lawie is a drafting assistant. The advocate is responsible for legal accuracy and filing.

**Can Lawie draft in Hindi?**
Not yet. Drafts are in English today. Hindi drafting: coming soon.

**Can I ask Lawie something outside drafting and using Lawie?**
This help covers using Lawie and what it is for. For anything else, write to contact@lawie.in.
