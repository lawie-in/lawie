## The five organic channels

1. **Founder on LinkedIn and X.** He is the best channel we have. Launch post, then 3 posts a week from calendar-week1/2 (Madhuri drafts, Ajay-checked claims). Reply to every comment in 24 hours.
2. **Bar-association demos.** 20 minutes live on the founder's phone, fictional matter, then a sign-up link with a UTM per association. Target 30% demo-to-sign-up. Ask the bar for a slot; never pitch inside a case.
3. **WhatsApp, opt-in only.** Broadcast list from demos and the site form. 2 broadcasts a week at most. STOP honoured in 24 hours. Opt-in record per number (date, source). No cold adds, no group spam.
4. **Referral codes.** Every demo attendee gets their code. Each referral gives +5 / +3 Ink as built. Say it only as Ajay and Vikram signed it.
5. **Law-college and junior-advocate groups.** Founder or a friendly junior shares the R3 reel ("You stay the advocate") in groups they already belong to. No cold adds.
