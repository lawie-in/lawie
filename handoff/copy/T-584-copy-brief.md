## Rules (Ajay, Meera; unchanged)
Workflow, never outcome. No "never miss", "court-ready", "accurate", "legally valid", "guaranteed", no time saved, no comparisons, no client-getting, no testimonials about results. "Ink a month", never draft counts or "unlimited". Sample drafts are fictional and say so. The product writes a first draft; the advocate reviews and files.
