# T-358: deleting never-verified email accounts (signed)

Signed: Ajay (CLO), 12 Oct 2026 IST.

| AC 5 item | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Period | **30 days after sign-up**, for email and password accounts whose email was never verified. Confirmed. Basis: DPDP Act 2023, s.8(7) (erase once the purpose is no longer served). |
| Privacy Policy | Yes, add one bullet to §9 "How Long We Keep Data", straight after the "Everything else" bullet, in the same release as T-358: `Accounts whose email address is never verified: deleted 30 days after sign-up.` |
| Version | Bump `CURRENT_PRIVACY_VERSION` and "Last updated". **No** signed summary for this version, so no T-343 prompt. The change only shortens retention, so nobody needs to re-accept. |
| Deletion | Same path as account erasure (T-187 ledger rule included). Payment records are not affected, because a never-verified account cannot reach checkout. |
