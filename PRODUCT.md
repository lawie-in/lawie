# Lawie: product in one page

## Who it is for
Advocates in Indian district courts, working mostly on phones and older laptops, often on slow connections, in English and Hindi. Many are sole practitioners who draft a few documents a day and pay for it themselves. They are busy, sceptical of software, and responsible for every word they file.

## What it does
Lawie helps an advocate turn a description of a matter into a first draft of a court document, which the advocate reviews, edits and files. It is a drafting tool, not legal advice, and the advocate stays responsible for the final document. Ink is the unit of use: plans give "N Ink a month", and a draft spends Ink.

## What each surface is for
- **Landing and pricing:** let an advocate see what Lawie does and start a free draft in under a minute. Claims describe the workflow only, never outcomes.
- **Sign-up and login:** get in quickly; clear errors; no surprises about email or privacy.
- **Dashboard ("Your shelf"):** where an advocate sees their recent documents, Ink, and plan, and starts the next draft.
- **New draft (describe-first):** describe the matter in plain words; Lawie asks only what it needs; the cost in Ink is shown before spending.
- **Draft ready and editor:** read, edit, finalise and export. Nothing is hidden about what was drafted and what needs checking.
- **Settings:** profile, plan (choose, status, cancel), privacy, account.

## Rules for every screen
- Plain words; Hindi where it exists. Never say or imply "unlimited", "court-ready", "legally valid", or promise outcomes or time saved.
- No dead buttons. Every screen has loading, empty and error states.
- Never show a number or message that is not true in the build (for example, Ink "used" when none was).
- Matter text and personal data never appear in URLs, logs or analytics.
- Mobile first at 360 px; works with reduced motion and with the tab hidden.
