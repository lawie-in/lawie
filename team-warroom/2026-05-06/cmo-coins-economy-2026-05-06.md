# CMO take — Coins economy for Lawie

**Final call: Ship coins POST-Phase-1. Keep ₹799/mo unlimited as the hero pitch until we hit 25 paying users. Then layer coins as a referral + pay-as-you-go SKU.**

---

## 1. ICP fit
Partial fit. Advocates are price-sensitive and will absolutely chase a coin discount — same brain that haggles ₹20 at a chai stall. But the analogy isn't Candy Crush; it's Paytm cashback or Jio recharge packs, both of which Bihar/Jharkhand advocates use daily. Coins don't cheapen if framed as "credits" or "tokens" — never call them coins in Hindi UI; call them "drafts" or "credits." "Coin" sounds like a game.

## 2. Coin fatigue risk — REAL
Yes, this kills conversion. A junior advocate at 10pm filing bail does NOT want to math out "10 coins x ₹X = is this worth it." Subscription wins on cognitive load. Every coin question is a churn moment. If we ship coins, the default path on the homepage MUST stay "₹799/mo, unlimited drafts" — coins are the side door.

## 3. Where coins genuinely unlock growth
- **Referrals — REAL lift.** District court bars are gossip networks; "earn 50 free credits per referral" is concrete and shareable on WhatsApp.
- **Pay-as-you-go capture — REAL lift.** 60% of advocates I'd bet won't commit ₹799/mo upfront but will drop ₹199 for 20 drafts. This is the bigger unlock than referral.
- **Stickiness via loss aversion — MARGINAL.** Indian advocates aren't loss-averse on small balances; they'll let coins expire.
- **Word-of-mouth — REAL but downstream of referral mechanic.** Don't double-count.

## 4. Architecture call
**Subscription-primary, coins as the pay-as-you-go SKU and referral currency.** Two SKUs, but subscription is the headline; coins are the on-ramp for hesitant users and the rail for referral rewards. Coins-primary makes us look like a recharge app, not a legal product. Brand cost is too high.

## 5. Referral ceiling for our ICP
Realistic ceiling: **12-18% of paying users refer at least one advocate**. Higher than B2B SaaS (5-10%) because of dense bar association networks; lower than Cred (consumer FOMO doesn't apply). Don't promise the founder 30%.

## 7. Trial reframe — coins vs drafts
**WORSE.** "10 drafts free, 5 + 5 earned by rating" is plain English. "100 coins, 10 coins per draft, 5 coins per rating" forces a multiplication step before the user has seen value. Keep SCRUM-59 as drafts. Move to coin language only after trial conversion.

## 8. A/B test post-launch
**Test:** Homepage pricing — "₹799/mo unlimited" only vs "₹799/mo unlimited + ₹199 draft pack" alternative.
**Metric:** Trial signup-to-paid rate at day 14.
**Decision rule:** Ship draft pack only if it lifts paid conversion by ≥15% AND doesn't drop ARPU by more than 10%. Otherwise kill it — the optionality isn't worth the complexity tax.

## 9. Brand risk
**Mild risk if rushed, neutral if delayed.** Shipping coins in Phase 1 (still proving credibility with 0 paying users) makes us look like a recharge app to a serious profession. Shipping post-25-users, framed as "credits for advocates who don't want a subscription," reads as mature pricing. Wait.
